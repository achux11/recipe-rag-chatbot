Title: Indian Chicken Korma

Cuisine: Mughlai / North Indian
Type: Rich Curry
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 600 g chicken, bone-in pieces
- 2 onions, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 cup yogurt, whisked
- 10–12 cashew nuts or almonds, soaked
- 1 tsp poppy seeds (optional)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1.5 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
- 1 bay leaf
- 3–4 green cardamom pods
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- Salt to taste
- 1/2 cup water (or as needed)
- 2 tbsp fresh cream (optional)
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps:
1. Blend soaked cashews/almonds and poppy seeds with a little water into a smooth paste and set aside.
2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add bay leaf, cinnamon, and green cardamom. Sauté until aromatic.
3. Add sliced onions and sauté on medium heat until golden brown.
4. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1–2 minutes until raw smell disappears.
5. Add chicken pieces and sauté on medium-high heat until they turn white and lightly browned.
6. Lower the heat. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
7. Add yogurt little by little, stirring continuously so it doesn’t curdle.
8. Add the cashew/almond paste and mix thoroughly.
9. Add water to adjust gravy consistency. Cover and cook on low-medium heat for 20–25 minutes until chicken is tender.
10. Once the chicken is cooked, add garam masala and cardamom powder. Simmer for 2–3 minutes.
11. Optionally, stir in fresh cream for extra richness.
12. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.

Serving:
- Best served with naan, paratha, or jeera rice.

Notes:
- Korma should have a mildly spiced, creamy gravy; adjust chili level accordingly.
