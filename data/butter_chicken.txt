Title: Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Cuisine: North Indian
Type: Main Course
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 600 g boneless chicken (preferably thigh), cut into medium pieces
- 3 tbsp yogurt (curd)
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
- Salt to taste

For the gravy:
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 medium onions, sliced
- 3 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 10–12 cashew nuts
- 2 green chilies (optional)
- 1 tsp sugar or honey
- 1/2 cup fresh cream
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (for color)
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Additional 1 tbsp butter and 1 tsp kasuri methi for finishing

Marination Steps:
1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and crushed kasuri methi.
2. Add chicken pieces and coat well. Marinate for at least 1 hour (preferably 4 hours or overnight in the fridge).

Gravy Steps:
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp butter in a pan. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden.
4. Add tomatoes, cashews, and green chilies. Cook until tomatoes are soft and oil begins to separate.
5. Cool the mixture slightly and blend into a smooth paste (add a little water if needed).

Cooking Steps:
6. In another pan, cook marinated chicken on medium-high heat until lightly charred and almost cooked. You can also grill or bake the chicken.
7. In a clean pan, heat 1 tbsp butter. Add the prepared onion-tomato-cashew paste.
8. Add Kashmiri red chili powder, sugar/honey, salt, and a little water to adjust consistency. Cook for 5–7 minutes on low heat.
9. Add the cooked chicken pieces and simmer for 8–10 minutes so they absorb the flavors.
10. Lower the heat and add fresh cream, garam masala, and crushed kasuri methi. Mix gently; do not boil after adding cream.
11. Finish with a small cube of butter on top.

Serving:
- Serve hot with naan, roti, or jeera rice.

Notes:
- Adjust cream quantity to make it lighter or richer.
- Use Kashmiri chili powder for color without too much heat.
