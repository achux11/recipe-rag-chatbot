Title: Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Cuisine: Hyderabadi / Indian
Type: One Pot Main Course
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 500 g chicken, bone-in pieces
- 2 cups basmati rice, soaked for 30 minutes
- 3 large onions, thinly sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 cup thick yogurt
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 3–4 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 cup chopped mint leaves
- 1/2 cup chopped coriander leaves
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 2 tsp biryani masala or garam masala
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 4 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Whole spices: 2 bay leaves, 4 cloves, 4 green cardamom, 1 black cardamom, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 1 star anise
- Salt to taste
- Saffron strands soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk (optional)

Marination Steps:
1. In a large bowl, mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, biryani masala, salt, lemon juice, half of the mint and coriander leaves, and half of the slit green chilies.
2. Marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight in the fridge for best flavor).

Rice Preparation:
3. Boil plenty of water in a large pot. Add salt and whole spices (bay leaves, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, star anise).
4. Add soaked and drained basmati rice. Cook until 70% done (the grain should still be firm in the center).
5. Drain the rice and keep aside.

Chicken Masala:
6. Heat oil and ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot. Fry sliced onions until golden and crisp. Reserve half for layering (fried onions).
7. In the remaining onions in the pot, add tomatoes and cook until soft.
8. Add marinated chicken along with the marinade. Cook on medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes until chicken is partially cooked and masala thickens.

Layering:
9. Reduce heat to low. Spread the chicken masala evenly at the bottom of the pot.
10. Layer the par-boiled rice on top of the chicken.
11. Sprinkle remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, green chilies, and saffron milk over the rice.
12. Drizzle 1 tbsp ghee on top.

Dum Cooking:
13. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid. You can seal the edges with dough to trap steam.
14. Cook on very low heat for 20–25 minutes (dum cooking) so the chicken cooks fully and rice absorbs all flavors.
15. Let the biryani rest for 10 minutes before opening the lid. Gently fluff from the sides without breaking the rice grains.

Serving:
- Serve hot with raita, salad, and lemon wedges.

Notes:
- Use good quality long-grain basmati rice for best texture.
- Adjust chili levels based on preference.
