Title: Tandoori Chicken

Cuisine: North Indian
Type: Appetizer / Main
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 1 kg chicken, cut into large pieces with slits (leg and thigh pieces preferred)
- 1/2 cup thick yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1.5 tsp red chili powder (or Kashmiri chili powder for color)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1.5 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala or tandoori masala
- 1 tbsp mustard oil (or any cooking oil)
- Salt to taste
- Red food color (optional, a pinch)

Steps:
1. Clean and pat dry the chicken pieces. Make deep slits on each piece so the marinade penetrates.
2. In a large bowl, mix yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, cumin powder, coriander powder, garam masala, mustard oil, salt, and optional food color.
3. Add chicken pieces to the bowl and coat them well with the marinade, making sure the marinade goes into the slits.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight for best flavor.
5. Preheat oven to 220°C (430°F). Line a tray with foil and lightly grease a grill rack.
6. Place marinated chicken pieces on the rack. Bake for 20–25 minutes, turning once halfway, until chicken is cooked and slightly charred at edges.
7. Optionally, finish with 2–3 minutes of high broil/grill for extra char.
8. Brush with melted butter or ghee before serving.

Serving:
- Serve hot with onion rings, lemon wedges, mint chutney, and naan.

Notes:
- You can also cook this on a charcoal grill or tandoor for authentic smoky flavor.
- Adjust chili powder quantity to make it mild or spicy.
