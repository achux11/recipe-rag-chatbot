Title: Chicken 65

Cuisine: South Indian
Type: Snack / Starter
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 500 g boneless chicken, small cubes
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1.5 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp rice flour or cornflour
- Salt to taste
- Oil for deep frying

For Tempering:
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1 tbsp yogurt (optional, for coating)

Marination:
1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, lemon juice, rice flour/cornflour, and salt to make a thick marinade.
2. Add chicken pieces and coat evenly. Marinate for at least 1 hour.

Frying:
3. Heat oil in a deep pan on medium-high heat.
4. Fry the marinated chicken pieces in batches until golden and crisp. Do not overcrowd the pan.
5. Drain on paper towels to remove excess oil.

Tempering:
6. In another pan, heat 2 tbsp oil. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
7. Add curry leaves and slit green chilies. Sauté for a few seconds.
8. Optional: Add 1 tbsp yogurt and mix quickly on low heat to form a light coating sauce.
9. Add the fried chicken pieces and toss well so they are coated with the tempering.

Serving:
- Serve hot as a snack with onion rings and lemon wedges.

Notes:
- Adjust chili powder as needed; Chicken 65 is usually quite spicy.
