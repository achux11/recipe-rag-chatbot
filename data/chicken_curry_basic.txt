Title: Basic Indian Chicken Curry

Cuisine: Indian
Type: Main Course
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 500 g chicken (bone-in or boneless, cut into pieces)
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, pureed or finely chopped
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1.5 tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- 1.5 cups water

Steps:
1. Heat oil in a pan on medium heat. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Sauté for 1–2 minutes until raw smell goes away.
3. Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates from the masala.
4. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Cook the spices for 1–2 minutes on low heat.
5. Add chicken pieces and mix well so they are coated with the masala. Sear for 3–4 minutes.
6. Add salt and water. Mix, cover, and cook on medium-low heat for 15–20 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through.
7. Once the chicken is tender and gravy has thickened, sprinkle garam masala and simmer for 2 minutes.
8. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with rice or roti.

Notes:
- For richer gravy, you can add 2 tbsp of yogurt after frying the spices.
- Adjust water based on how thick or thin you like the curry.
