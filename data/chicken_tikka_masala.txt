Title: Chicken Tikka Masala

Cuisine: Indian / Indo-British
Type: Curry
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 500 g boneless chicken, cut into cubes

For Tikka Marinade:
- 1/2 cup thick yogurt
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste

For Masala Gravy:
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 green chili, slit (optional)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder (or Kashmiri chili powder)
- 1.5 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 cup fresh cream
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

Marination & Tikka:
1. In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin powder, lemon juice, oil, and salt.
2. Add chicken cubes and coat well. Marinate for at least 1 hour (longer for better flavor).
3. Thread marinated chicken onto skewers or place on a baking tray.
4. Grill in a preheated oven at 220°C (430°F) for 12–15 minutes or until slightly charred and cooked. Alternatively, pan-fry or air-fry until done.

Masala Gravy:
5. Heat oil and butter in a pan. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
6. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chili. Sauté for 1–2 minutes.
7. Add tomato puree and cook until the raw smell goes and oil starts to separate.
8. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook the masala on low heat for 3–4 minutes.
9. Add a little water if needed to prevent burning and to adjust consistency.

Combine:
10. Add the grilled chicken tikka pieces to the masala gravy and mix well.
11. Simmer for 5–7 minutes so the chicken absorbs the flavors.
12. Lower heat and add fresh cream and garam masala. Stir gently.
13. Garnish with coriander leaves.

Serving:
- Serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

Notes:
- For a smoky flavor, you can apply the dhungar (charcoal smoke) technique at the end.
