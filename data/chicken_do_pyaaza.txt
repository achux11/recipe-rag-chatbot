Title: Chicken Do Pyaaza

Cuisine: North Indian
Type: Curry
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 600 g chicken, curry cut
- 4 onions (2 finely chopped, 2 cut into petals)
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1.5 tsp red chili powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps:
1. Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Sauté for 1–2 minutes.
3. Add tomatoes and cook until soft and oil separates.
4. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Cook the masala for 2–3 minutes.
5. Add chicken pieces and mix well. Cook on medium heat for 5–7 minutes until chicken changes color and is partially cooked.
6. Add onion petals and mix gently so they remain slightly crunchy later.
7. Add a little water (about 1/2–3/4 cup) to form a gravy. Cover and cook on low-medium heat for 15–20 minutes until chicken is tender.
8. Once the chicken is cooked and gravy has thickened, add garam masala and simmer for 2 minutes.
9. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.

Serving:
- Serve with roti, naan, or steamed rice.

Notes:
- “Do Pyaaza” means double onions; the dish uses onions both in the gravy and as chunks.
