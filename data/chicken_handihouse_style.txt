Title: Dhaba Style Chicken Handi

Cuisine: North Indian / Dhaba Style
Type: Curry
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 700 g chicken, curry cut
- 3 onions, finely chopped
- 3 tomatoes, pureed
- 2 tbsp oil + 1 tbsp ghee
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 cup thick yogurt
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 2.5 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps:
1. Heat oil and ghee in a handi or heavy-bottomed pot.
2. Add chopped onions and sauté on medium heat until deep golden brown.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Sauté for 2 minutes.
4. Add tomato puree and cook on medium heat until the masala thickens and oil separates.
5. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Cook on low heat for 2–3 minutes.
6. Add chicken pieces and mix well so they are coated with the masala. Sear for 5–7 minutes.
7. In a bowl, whisk yogurt and slowly add it to the pot, stirring continuously to avoid curdling.
8. Add salt and a little water to adjust gravy consistency. Cover and cook on low-medium heat for 20–25 minutes until chicken is fully cooked.
9. Once the chicken is tender and oil floats on top, add garam masala and crushed kasuri methi.
10. Simmer for 3–4 minutes on low heat.
11. Garnish with coriander leaves before serving.

Serving:
- Serve hot with tandoori roti, naan, or jeera rice.

Notes:
- Slow cooking in a handi (or heavy pot) gives authentic dhaba-style flavor.
