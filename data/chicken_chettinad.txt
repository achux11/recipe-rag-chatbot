Title: Chicken Chettinad

Cuisine: South Indian (Chettinad)
Type: Spicy Curry
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- 600 g chicken, curry cut
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- Salt to taste

For Chettinad Masala:
- 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 4–5 dried red chilies
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 4 cloves
- 2 green cardamom
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- 2 tbsp grated coconut

Masala Preparation:
1. In a pan, dry roast coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, dried red chilies, peppercorns, cloves, cardamom, and cinnamon on low heat until aromatic.
2. Add grated coconut and roast until it turns light golden.
3. Cool the mixture, then grind to a smooth paste with a little water. Set aside.

Curry Steps:
4. Heat oil in a pan. Add curry leaves and sauté for a few seconds.
5. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden.
6. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1–2 minutes.
7. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and oil starts to separate.
8. Add chicken pieces, salt, and sauté for 4–5 minutes.
9. Add the prepared Chettinad masala paste and mix well.
10. Add enough water to get desired gravy consistency.
11. Cover and cook on low-medium heat for 20–25 minutes until chicken is cooked and flavors are well combined.

Serving:
- Serve hot with steamed rice, dosa, idli, or parotta.

Notes:
- Adjust the number of dried red chilies and peppercorns based on spice tolerance.
